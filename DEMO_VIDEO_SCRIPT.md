# Bizmate AI Demo Video Script

Target length: 2 minutes

## Recording Setup

- Open the app at `http://127.0.0.1:5173/`
- Start from the sign-in screen
- Use `Try demo` so the video does not depend on Firebase login
- Record with Windows Game Bar: `Win + Alt + R`
- Speak slowly and keep the mouse movement simple

## 2-Minute Script

### 0:00-0:10 - Intro

Say:

> Hi everyone, this is Bizmate AI, a small business command center for shop owners to manage sales, stock, expenses, staff attendance, payroll, and customer bills from one place.

Show:

- Show the sign-in screen
- Move the cursor to `Try demo`
- Click `Try demo`

### 0:10-0:25 - Problem

Say:

> Many small shops still use notebooks, calculators, or separate apps for daily work. This makes it easy to miss sales, forget expenses, run out of stock, or calculate staff salary incorrectly.

Show:

- Let the dashboard load
- Slowly move over the dashboard cards
- Keep the screen steady while speaking

### 0:25-0:40 - Dashboard

Say:

> Bizmate AI brings the important business information into one dashboard. The owner can quickly see sales, expenses, inventory items, low stock alerts, and staff details.

Show:

- Point to total sales
- Point to total expenses
- Point to low stock and staff summary

### 0:40-0:58 - Stock Management

Say:

> In the stock section, the owner can add products with quantity, purchase price, selling price, and reorder level. If an item is low in stock, the app highlights it clearly.

Show:

- Click `Stock`
- Show existing stock items
- Click `Add item`
- Briefly show the fields
- Close the modal
- Point to a low stock or in stock label

### 0:58-1:20 - Record Sale With Auto Price

Say:

> In sales, the owner can select an item directly from stock. Bizmate AI automatically fills the selling price. After entering quantity, the total bill amount is calculated automatically.

Show:

- Click `Sales`
- Click `Add sale`
- Enter an invoice number like `INV-2001`
- Select an item from the item dropdown
- Enter quantity `2`
- Show the unit price and total amount auto-filled
- Click `Save sale`

### 1:20-1:35 - Generate Bill

Say:

> After saving the sale, Bizmate AI instantly creates a printable bill with the shop details, invoice number, item, quantity, rate, and total amount.

Show:

- Show the bill popup
- Move the cursor over the invoice number and total
- Move the cursor to `Print`
- Close the bill

### 1:35-1:50 - Expenses

Say:

> The expenses section helps the owner record costs like supplier payments, rent, electricity, and delivery charges, so business spending stays organized.

Show:

- Click `Expenses`
- Show expense records
- Click `Add expense`
- Briefly show category, amount, vendor, and notes fields
- Close the modal

### 1:50-2:08 - Staff Attendance and Salary

Say:

> In the staff section, the owner can add staff members with their daily wage, then mark them present or absent. The app counts present days, absent days, and calculates salary payable.

Show:

- Click `Staff`
- Show present days, absent days, daily wage, and salary
- Click `Present` or `Absent` for one staff member
- Show the updated attendance pill

### 2:08-2:20 - Extra Features and Closing

Say:

> Bizmate AI also supports multiple shops, dark mode, search, export, print, and remove options for sales, expenses, stock, staff, and shops. Demo Mode makes the project easy for judges to test instantly. Thank you for watching.

Show:

- Point to the shop selector
- Click dark mode once
- Point to search/export/print
- End on the dashboard

## Shorter Closing If You Need Exactly 2 Minutes

If the video is getting too long, replace the final section with:

> Bizmate AI helps small shop owners save time, reduce mistakes, and manage their business from one simple app. Thank you for watching.

Show:

- End on the dashboard

# Bizmate AI Demo Video Script

Target length: 75-90 seconds

## Recording Setup

- Open the app at `http://127.0.0.1:5173/`
- Start from the sign-in screen
- Use Demo Mode so the video does not depend on Firebase
- Record with Windows Game Bar: `Win + Alt + R`

## Script

### 0:00-0:10 - Intro

Say:

> This is Bizmate AI, a command center for small shop owners to manage sales, inventory, expenses, staff attendance, payroll, and billing from one place.

Show:

- Sign-in screen
- Click `Try demo`

### 0:10-0:25 - Dashboard

Say:

> The dashboard gives the owner a quick view of sales, expenses, net profit, low stock, and recent activity.

Show:

- Dashboard metrics
- Recent activity
- Inventory watch

### 0:25-0:45 - Inventory to Sale

Say:

> When recording a sale, the owner can choose an inventory item. Bizmate automatically fills the selling price and calculates the total based on quantity.

Show:

- Go to `Stock`
- Point out item selling price
- Go to `Sales`
- Click `Add sale`
- Select an item
- Enter quantity
- Show total amount auto-updating
- Save sale

### 0:45-0:58 - Bill Generation

Say:

> After saving a sale, Bizmate instantly generates a printable bill for the customer.

Show:

- Bill modal
- Invoice number, item, quantity, rate, total
- Click or hover near `Print`
- Close bill

### 0:58-1:15 - Staff Attendance and Payroll

Say:

> Staff attendance is tracked in a payroll-friendly way. The app shows present days, absent days, daily wage, and salary payable.

Show:

- Go to `Staff`
- Click `Present` or `Absent`
- Show present/absent counts and salary payable

### 1:15-1:25 - Demo Reliability

Say:

> For hackathon judging, Demo Mode works without login or backend setup. For real use, the app also supports Firebase Authentication and Firestore.

Show:

- Demo mode pill in header
- Dark mode toggle briefly

### 1:25-1:30 - Closing

Say:

> Bizmate AI helps small businesses spend less time managing scattered records and more time running the shop.

Show:

- End on dashboard

## Devpost Caption

Bizmate AI is a small-business command center for shop owners. It manages inventory, sales, printable bills, expenses, staff attendance, and salary calculation. The app includes Firebase support for real deployments and a no-login Demo Mode for reliable hackathon judging.
